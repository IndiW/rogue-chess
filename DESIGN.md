# Visual system
Experience mode: a dungeon chess table with an immediately playable central board. Moss and parchment pixel pieces, charcoal green surroundings, amber relics. Desktop: expedition path / board / inventory. Mobile: condensed run strip, four fixed item slots and selected-piece abilities above the full-width board. Pack counts reserve space and never shift the board. Square geometry and authored pixel silhouettes establish the game world.

Tokens: background #161c19, surface #1d271f, text #e9e9d7, muted #9aab9c, amber #e6b968, board light #c5ccb0, board dark #657e59. DM Sans for UI, Space Grotesk for titles. Clear legal move dots, last move squares, oracle highlights, keyboard focus rings. No decorative motion; board updates communicate turns.

Piece refinement: authored 15 × 19 silhouettes with a cross for the king, three-tined queen crown, split bishop mitre, horse-head knight, battlement rook, and shorter round pawn. Ivory palette: body #e8dfba, light #fff9e2, shade #b2a67e, detail #938563. Obsidian palette: body #344c49, light #8aaba0, shade #233a37, detail #142d2a. Offset drop shadows and small contact shadows anchor pieces on the board. Header audio controls use the existing square geometry and amber pressed state; collapse to accessible icon buttons on mobile.

Original A-minor chiptune: 108 BPM, 64-step lead, triangle bass and quiet square arpeggios. Independent music and effects controls with saved preferences; music enabled after a gesture on first visit, audio unlocked by user interaction, background music paused. A locally hosted MP3 uses the browser media channel; Web Audio supplies independent effects.

Relics: original indexed-color 16 × 18 pixel sprites: amethyst Echo shard, blue-glass Oracle lens, purple Hourglass with amber sand, crowned green Royal seed. Left-lit pixel shading and contact shadows match pieces. Time tokens: accent #cba6f4, border #79608f, surface #2a2434, secondary #cbb8dd. Insight: #8ed5f1 / #4e8095 / #1d303a / #b2d0dd. Growth: #b5d88f / #6b8751 / #293622 / #c3d5af. Both type label and color appear on reward and equipped states.

Player clock: next to player identity, tabular Space Grotesk numerals, 3:00 starting value. Active clock uses amber, paused uses moss, 30 seconds or less uses coral #ffb19b. No flashing or layout rerenders on ticks.

Run management: full main menu with angled starting-board artwork and original pixel pack, large Space Grotesk title, amber primary Start/Resume, distinct Restart and warm brown Quit controls. Resume summary names floor and remaining time; discard actions use confirmation dialogs. On mobile, prioritize controls and hide decorative menu board. Audio controls and compact help remain in the header; game adds a visible Menu control.

Pack experience: a sealed purple pixel pack bearing the earning piece opens into three equal reward cards, two piece talents and one general item in random order. Movement accent #f0bf7b, border #a07d4c, surface #342c20, secondary #dec9ac; Time/Insight/Growth inherit existing tokens. Original piece sprites with small authored SVG runes identify talents. Mobile cards stack with art on the left and readable effect text on the right. A 900ms seal-breaking animation with pixel sparks precedes staggered card reveals; selecting a reward locks all choices during a 380ms confirmation. Reduced motion removes travel. A dedicated green-ledger rewards room between battles contains sealed packs, claimed receipts, next-floor items and a sticky advance control. Selected-piece capture track and charge descriptions show actual state. Tiny gold square rank markers appear on upgraded board pieces.

Checkmate sequence: stepped king topple and brief board impact, then a full-width skewed blue #238dcc ribbon with gold #ffe079 #1 and ivory CHECKMATE lettering. Blue structural edge #155381; defeat variant #914558 with #562b3d edge. This deliberate arcade treatment follows the user's Victory Royale reference. Banner auto-continues to rewards/K.O., with an explicit Continue button. Reduced motion removes shake/topple/reveal travel. Interaction surfaces use touch-action:manipulation; pinch zoom is preserved.
